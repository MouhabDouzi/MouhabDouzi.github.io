Research & Publications

From Utility Maximisation to Optimal Capital Market Line Allocation

SSRN Working Paper (ID 6623141), 2026. Derives the closed-form optimal risky-asset allocation along the Capital Market Line and calibrates it to 2019–2024 U.S. equity data. Quantifies a welfare cost of up to 9.1% from a naive 100% equity allocation, benchmarked against four portfolio construction methods.

Portfolio Theory Utility Maximisation Python
View on SSRN

Animal Spirits and the Price of Risk

SSRN Working Paper (ID 7267680), 2026. A behavioural macro-finance model where sentiment-driven risk premia are countercyclical (correlation −0.90) and forecast future output (correlation −0.65), amplifying recession frequency by about 73%. Submitted to the Bank of England–LSE Behavioural Macro-Finance Workshop.

Behavioural Finance Macro-Finance Python
View on SSRN
